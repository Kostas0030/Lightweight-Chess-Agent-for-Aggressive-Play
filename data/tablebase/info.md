# tablebase — Files Not Included

The Syzygy tablebase files are not included in this repository due to size limitations.

## What belongs here

Syzygy endgame tablebases — binary files used for perfect endgame play when ≤5 pieces remain on the board. The engine queries these files during MCTS to get exact Win/Draw/Loss results without running the neural networks.

## How to get the files

You can download the Syzygy tablebases for free from the official source:

- [Syzygy tablebases — 3-4-5 piece](https://syzygy-tables.info)

After downloading, place the `.rtbw` and `.rtbz` files in this folder.
